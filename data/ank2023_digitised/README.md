# Measurements of Ank et al. (2023), digitised from the published figures

M. Ank et al., Lithium-ion cells in automotive applications: Tesla 4680 cylindrical cell teardown and
characterization, J. Electrochem. Soc. 170, 120536 (2023), doi:10.1149/1945-7111/ad14d0, CC BY 4.0.
Their raw data are at mediaTUM (doi:10.14459/2023mp1725661) and are not redistributed here.

Vector digitisations of the figures named in each file header (exact to plot precision):

- `thermal_fig16_charge.csv`: can temperatures at cap, centre and bottom during the 1C and 2C CC charges,
  resampled to 10 s (CC durations 3039 s and 1303 s, from Figure 16a);
- `hppc_r10s_fig14.csv`: 10 s pulse resistances;
- `pocv_c20_fig13a.csv`: C/20 pseudo-OCV;
- `eis_fig12b_points.csv`, `eis_fig12b_summary.csv`: impedance spectra.

Used in paper 1 for the comparison and calibration of the NMC set.
