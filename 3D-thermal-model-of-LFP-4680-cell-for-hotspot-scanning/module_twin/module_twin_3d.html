Module: 23 × 9p BYD 4680 LFP cells; each cell has OCV, R + RC, and core/can temperatures.

Short: a resistance inside one cell; drains and heats it. Terminal signal by position from the 3-D sheet model; hotspot from the 3-D library.

Stage 1, GRU: per group, an observer splits load voltage from a slow offset; a GRU on the NPU flags the group losing charge (every second).

Stage 2, EIS: after a GRU alarm, the BMA7418 scans the group's 9 cells at 10 Hz and 1 kHz; the warmest (lowest ratio) is named.
