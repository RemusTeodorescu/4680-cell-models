What this is. A reference 23s9p module (one of four in the Tesla Model Y 4680 pack) filled with BYD FC4680 LFP cells, and a fast thermal twin of it. Each cell is two thermal nodes (core and can), cooled by serpentine ribbons between the rows that run out from the manifold and back. Heat is ohmic (I²R, R = 5 mΩ per cell) plus V²/Risc in a shorted cell. Two NTCs sit on the cells at the coolant inlet and outlet. Parameters come from the paper 1 parameter set where available: heat capacity 281 J/K and core-to-can resistance 0.49 K/W. The cell resistance, the ribbon conductance (1 W/K per side) and the cell-to-cell conduction (0.02 W/K) are estimates. This is not the 3-D model of paper 1.2. It shows what the module's sensors can and cannot see.
